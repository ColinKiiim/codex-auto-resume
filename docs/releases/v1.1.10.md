# CodexAutoResume v1.1.10

发布日期：2026-10-25（UTC+8）

## 中文

### 改进

- 首页最近进度支持滚轮和滚动条，保留本次运行历史；底部跟随新进度，翻阅旧记录时保留位置和选中文字
- 设置增加简体中文 / English，即时切换并保存语言；不改变目标、监测或自动恢复状态
- 预览说明改为“采样图”，按文字留足宽度；本轮计数与简短中文说明文案统一
- 新用户默认后台监测，去掉实验标识；已有明确保存的监测方式保留，普通启动仍只观察
- 保留系统原生窗口边框，客户端预览不再混入位图模拟的旧式标题栏

## English

### Improved

- Scroll through timestamped progress for the current session; new entries follow the bottom while older entries retain their viewport and text selection
- Switch between Chinese and English in Settings immediately; the preference is saved without changing monitoring, auto-resume or the target
- Clear Sample caption without ellipsis, session recovery counter and shorter Chinese UI copy
- Background monitoring defaults on for new users; existing saved choices remain, and ordinary startup still observes without clicking
- Preserve native system window chrome; client-area previews omit simulated legacy title bars
